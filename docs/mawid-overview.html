Mawid موعد

An appointment system for your clinic.

What Mawid does

Mawid takes the place of the paper appointment book. The secretary books appointments on a computer. The system then does three things:

  • It prints a paper slip for the patient.
  • It prints the schedule for the whole day, every morning.
  • It sends each patient a WhatsApp reminder before the appointment.

Mawid runs on one computer inside the clinic. There is no monthly fee. Your patient information stays in the clinic. It is not sent to any outside company.

How it is used

  1. Book the appointment

    The secretary chooses the type of visit. The system shows only the times that are long enough for that visit. A check-up needs 20 minutes. A root canal needs 90 minutes. So they show different times on the same day. A new patient can be added while booking.

  2. The slip prints

    The slip prints as soon as the booking is saved. Nobody has to press a print button. The slip shows the appointment details. It also has a QR code.

  3. Scan the QR code

    Point a phone camera at the QR code on the slip. The patient's record opens on the phone. At the same time, the computer at the desk shows the same patient.

  4. The patient record

    It shows the patient's name and phone number, their next appointment, and all their past visits. It is easy to read on a phone.

  5. The WhatsApp reminder

    The patient gets a WhatsApp message about 18 hours before the appointment. It comes from the clinic's WhatsApp number. It gives the day, the time, the clinic address, and the phone number to call to cancel or change the appointment.

  6. Problems are shown on the screen

    Sometimes the printer does not work. Sometimes a message cannot be sent to a patient. When this happens, the desk computer shows it on the screen. It gives the reason and the patient's phone number. The secretary can then call the patient.

  7. The morning schedule

    Every morning the system prints the whole day on one page. There is a wide empty column for your notes. Cancelled appointments are not printed.

  8. The statistics screen

    It shows how many appointments you had, how many reminders were sent, how many reminders failed, and how many patients cancelled or did not come. You choose the period of time you want to see.

Other things to know

  • The system plans the reminder when the appointment is booked. If the reminder time falls when the clinic is closed, the system sends it earlier. For example, an appointment on Monday morning gets its reminder on Saturday afternoon, not on Sunday when the clinic is closed.
  • In three cases the system does not send a reminder: the appointment already started, it is less than 3 hours away, or it was cancelled. The patient is shown on the desk screen instead, so the secretary can call.
  • Some phone numbers do not have WhatsApp. The system checks the number before it sends. Without this check, the system would say the patient was reminded, but the patient would get nothing.
  • You can use the system in Arabic or in English.
  • These are all settings: your clinic name, your working hours, the types of visit, how long each visit takes, and the words in the reminder message. We can change them without new software.
  • The system copies your data by itself. One copy stays on the clinic computer. One copy is kept outside the clinic, in a protected form. Each copy is checked after it is made.

What the clinic needs

A computer at the desk
It stays on during working hours. It needs a fixed address on the clinic network. If the address changes, the QR codes on papers you already printed stop working.
A printer
Any printer that the computer can already print to. You can also use a small receipt printer. Then each slip is a small strip of paper instead of a full A4 page.
A second WhatsApp number
Only for reminders. Not your main clinic number. We connect it one time at the desk.
Wifi
Any phone that scans the QR codes must be on the same wifi as the computer.
